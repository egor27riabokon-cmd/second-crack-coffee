Small-batch roastery & bar

Roasted until the second crack,
then not a second more.

We buy green, roast on a 12-kilo drum out back, and pour what's freshest. Six single-origin lots on rotation, never more than three weeks off the roaster.

Ground to order

Every cup starts here.

No pre-ground bags sitting on a shelf. Every order gets its own grind, dialed to the brew method, seconds before water touches it. It's slower than scooping from a hopper of stale grounds — that's the point.

Why we roast small

A drum, not a factory.

Everything we sell was green a few weeks ago. We roast in small batches so we can taste every one before it goes on the bar — if a lot doesn't cup well, it doesn't ship, full stop.

We pay above commodity price direct to the farms we buy from, and we print the numbers on every bag. No blends, no house filler — just the lot, named.

“The best cup we ever pulled was also the plainest — one farm, one process, roasted for what it actually was.” — Nadia Ostrowski, head roaster
